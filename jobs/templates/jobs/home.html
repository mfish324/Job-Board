How the filter works

No black box. Every point in every score traces to a documented signal — and we publish the methodology.

1
We watch employer hiring systems directly

Listings come from employer ATS platforms — not scraped aggregator soup. We track requisition IDs, repost patterns, and whether companies actually close the roles they post.

2
Twelve signals, one score

Freshness, salary transparency, repost detection, evergreen-language detection, company hiring velocity, and more — each grounded in published labor-market research. Scored 0–100, rescored daily.

3
Below 65 never gets published

Most listings we track don't make it onto this site. That's the product: not more jobs — fewer, better ones. Read the full methodology →
